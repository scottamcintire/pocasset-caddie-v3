The Hole 8 pond is real and now drawn; shorter trees and stronger shading are the options you'll actually notice

OpenStreetMap, our map source, never mapped this pond, so it wasn't drawn. The 2021 lidar shows it clearly: a basin about 28 × 44 m (≈1,000 m²) with a dead-flat floor at 5.0 m, 9 m below the tees. A flat floor like that is how lidar records water.

I traced the outline from the lidar. It's labelled approximate and unverified: the pond could have changed since 2021.

If you approve it, the pond goes in as its own locked "lidar-derived" source; the OpenStreetMap file is never edited. It will also appear in Hole 8's hazard list (yardages to reach and carry it). It stays marked unverified until you confirm it on the course.

Option A — shorter, thinner woods

Trees drawn about 8 m tall instead of 13, and only where the 2023 canopy is dense. Woods stay only where the imagery shows trees.

Catch: "thinner" removes small groups of trees, including the Hole 2 knoll you liked. If you want this direction, I'd try shorter only (8 m, same density): it keeps every group and still lets more ground show.

Option B — stronger ground shading

Deeper light and shadow on slopes. Heights and the 1.5× exaggeration don't change.

Only the strong version is noticeable. It would also deepen the contours on Hole 2 and every other hole.
